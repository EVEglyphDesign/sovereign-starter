# Sovereign Starter

The most basic boundary you can put around an AI surface. Three edges. Downloadable
as one Markdown file. Drop it into a new GitHub repository and your AI surface has
guardrails it did not have twenty minutes earlier.

## The triangle

- **Boot contract** — how the AI surface behaves before it does anything.
- **Canon** — how the artifact leaves the repository.
- **Sin registry** — how the truth of what happened is preserved.

Three vertices, three edges, no vertex removable. Take the boot contract away and the
surface drifts. Take the canon away and the artifact leaks. Take the sin registry
away and the other two become preferences instead of rules.

The triangle sits inside a circle. **The circle is the repository.** The AI surface
is a web browser — nothing you say to it is permanent, nothing you reason inside it
comes back to you once the session closes. The repository is where your reasoning is
yours. Facebook is beginning to give people their history back. AI surfaces are not
promising that yet. Until they do, the only way to keep what you think is to keep it
somewhere you hold.

You hold the repository. That is the whole idea. Everything else is mechanics.

## What this is not

- Not the sophisticated version. Not the enterprise version. Not the controlled EgD
  canon. The minimum shape that still works.
- Not opinionated about which AI surface you use. Toilet paper is toilet paper.
- Not permanent — you edit this file the moment it stops fitting.

More guardrails can be added on top later. The three edges are the floor.

## How to use it

### One-file path — recommended

1. Download [`SOVEREIGN-STARTER.md`](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/EVEglyphDesign/sovereign-starter/main/SOVEREIGN-STARTER.md).
2. Save it as `README.md` in the root of a new GitHub repository you control.
3. Open your AI surface and point it at the file.
4. Follow the two-mode setup in §0. Guided is five questions. Defaults is zero.

### Starter-pack path

Clone [this repository](https://github.com/EVEglyphDesign/sovereign-starter) as a
template. You get the same content laid out the way your repository will look:
`docs/BOOT-CONTRACT-STARTER.md`, `registry/DEFECTS.md`, and the landing page under
`docs/index.html`. Rename, edit, push.
